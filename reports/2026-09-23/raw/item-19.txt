Agensh is a scalable, self-organized multi-agent harness without a central orchestrator. Concurrent workers gather context, claim sub-tasks, share findings, verify, and merge progress asynchronously through a lightweight agentic organization infrastructure, consisting of a shared workspace, a message interface, and shared context.
