Synopsis
OpenSpec is a lightweight and configurable framework for creating and managing software
 specifications.
With OpenSpec, you capture what you want to build in a spec and keep your team and coding
 agents aligned as the work evolves. We help you refine the requirements, validate that they
 describe the right thing, and verify that the implementation matches.
In essence, OpenSpec helps you
build the right thing and build it right
.
