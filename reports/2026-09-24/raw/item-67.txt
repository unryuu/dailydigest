We introduce StudentBench, a suite of AI teaching evaluations and a public platform for measuring how well AI helps people learn. The paper compares AI tutoring, expert human tutoring, and a no tutoring control on Quantitative and Verbal GRE learning gains, and evaluates AI-generated lesson plans and practice problems.
Dataset:
https://huggingface.co/datasets/handshake-ai-research/studentbench
Project:
https://studentbench.org
Code:
https://github.com/Handshake-AI-Research/studentbench

This is the first work to establish and measure the cost to augment a student’s learning by one percentage point.
Over 1 trillion USD is being spent teaching machines to improve machines.
StudentBench shows the value of teaching machines to improve humans.
